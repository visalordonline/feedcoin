Official Operating Policies

Terms, Yield Rules & Withdrawal Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • FeedCoin Operating Guidelines

1. Daily Mining & Reward Terms

FeedCoin grants registered members access to daily micro-rewards. The daily tap reward generates 200 FeedCoin once per 24-hour cycle. Any automated bot interactions, emulator abuse, or multiple account exploits will lead to immediate account suspension and forfeit of accrued points.

2. Investment Pools & Yield Disclosures

FeedCoin provides access to fixed Naira yield options ranging from ₦5,000 to ₦4,000,000. Daily advertised yield (up to 4.5% daily) calculates across scheduled business allocations. Capital enrolled into fixed yield terms remains committed for the duration of the cycle; premature liquidations forfeit unpaid yields.

3. Capital Deployment & Fund Management

Capital deposited into plans is deployed toward small enterprise debt financing, verified merchant equipment purchases, and revenue-sharing retail contracts. Returns depend on underlying loan settlements and operational margins. Users are advised to participate within prudent financial limits.

4. Saturday-Only Withdrawal Policy

To preserve settlement liquidity and maintain security auditing across all outbound transactions, the following strict terms apply:

  • Processing Day: Withdrawals are processed exclusively on Saturdays.
  • Submission Timelines: Requests may be submitted anytime during the week. Approved requests are queued and batched every Saturday morning.
  • Minimum Limits: Task and bonus balances require a minimum threshold of ₦2,000 before a withdrawal can be logged.
  • Banking Accuracy: Account names must match your registered account profile. Incorrect bank details will delay payout processing to the following Saturday cycle.

5. Corporate Information & Licensing

FeedCoin operates in association with EMERSON SOFTWARE SERVICES. While holding foreign commercial business identification, micro-earning activities and peer investment pools remain subject to local platform guidelines and administrative oversight.
